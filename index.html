Our Mission

The mission of the MUBAS Seventh-day Adventist Club is to preach the everlasting Gospel of salvation by grace through faith in Jesus Christ, maintain Adventist Christian standards as upheld by the Adventist faith, provide a forum for enhancing knowledge of the Bible and Adventist faith in the light of the Bible and Spirit of Prophecy, provide opportunities to members to carry out such roles as will prepare them for usefulness in the mainstream Church and promote love, fellowship and unity among members.
